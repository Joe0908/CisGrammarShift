# Real-data analysis status

This file originally proposed a pluripotency-factor ChIP-nexus extension of the synthetic benchmark. That
route has been superseded by a CAP-SELEX × GHT-SELEX × ChIP-seq study with hTSC follow-up. The historical
analysis is complete, but audit identified ChIP-informed locus construction; its numerical results are now
legacy sensitivities. The official GHT-only fixed-tile rerun is also complete: only one of four primary TFs
passed the frozen effect gate. A focused TGIF2–GCM1 external-context screen was directionally consistent but
failed its effect gate in both trophoblast states. A full fixed-genome universe remains useful for a future
methods paper, but cannot reverse either predeclared decision.

The current plans and results are separated deliberately:

- [`capselex_feasibility.md`](capselex_feasibility.md): directed-pair feasibility and shortcut diagnosis;
- [`genomic_targeting_phase0.md`](genomic_targeting_phase0.md): revised primary design and official results;
- [`trophoblast_validation.md`](trophoblast_validation.md): external-outcome occupancy association and
  functional tests;
- [`data_access.md`](data_access.md): exact public sources and prohibited circular assets.
- [`research_audit_2026-08-10.md`](research_audit_2026-08-10.md): publication decision and remaining gaps.

The original synthetic benchmark remains a controlled positive test. It is not presented as experimental
evidence.
